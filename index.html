JETPACK JONES

Explore the industrial complex on foot, sprint across catwalks, leap over rotating cogs, and fire your jetpack to soar across high-voltage hazards!

🎮 KEYBOARD CONTROLS:
• [A] / [D] or [Left] / [Right] : Walk Left / Right
• [Shift] (Hold) : Sprint / Run
• [W] / [Up Arrow] : Jump
• [Space] (Hold) or in mid-air : 🔥 Fire Jetpack Flight
• [J] / [Enter] / [Click] : 🔫 Shoot Blaster
• [R] : 🤸 Acrobatic Stunt Ragdoll Tumble

📱 MOBILE TOUCH CONTROLS:
• [◀] / [▶] Walk  |  [⚡] Run  |  [⬆] Jump  |  [🔥] Jetpack Fly
